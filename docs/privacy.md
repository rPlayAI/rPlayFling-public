# rPlayFling privacy policy

Effective October 13, 2026. rPlayFling is made by VMLite Corporation.

**In short: rPlayFling collects no personal data.** There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Nothing you do in the app is sent to us.

## What stays on your iPhone

- **Web browsing.** rPlayFling is a web browser. The sites you open receive what any browser sends them, and their cookies and logins are stored on your iPhone, as in Safari. Those sites' own privacy policies apply to them.
- **Bookmarks, favorites, recent casts and settings** are stored on your iPhone only.
- **The app's log** stays on your iPhone. It leaves only if you choose Share Log File and send it to someone yourself.

## Permissions, and what each is used for

- **Local network:** to find TVs, speakers and other receivers, and to stream the video you cast to them. The stream goes straight from your iPhone to the device on your network.
- **Location (while CarPlay is connected):** only to tell whether the car is moving, so that video on the car screen can be hidden while driving. Your location is never stored or sent anywhere.
- **Photos:** to show photos and videos you choose on the car screen or a TV. Nothing is copied or uploaded. Saving to Photos happens only when you choose Add to Photos.
- **Microphone and speech recognition:** for voice input on the car screen, to say a website or a search. Speech is turned into text by Apple's speech recognition service under Apple's privacy policy.
- **Camera:** only when a web page you visit asks for it, for example to upload a photo. rPlayFling itself never uses the camera.

## Linking with other apps

If you link a video app to rPlayFling with a TV code, rPlayFling talks to that app's service the way a TV does, so that the app can send videos to your car. That exchange is between your iPhone and that service.

## Purchases

The rPlayFling Unlimited purchase is handled entirely by Apple. rPlayFling only learns whether the purchase is active; we never see your payment details.

## Children

rPlayFling is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

## Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page. Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/rPlayAI/rPlayFling-public/issues
